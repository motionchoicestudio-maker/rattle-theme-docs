Rattle

A Shopify theme for brands with a small, focused catalog. This guide covers setup, every section and the theme settings.

Getting started

  1. In Shopify admin open Online Store → Themes, add Rattle and click Customize.
  2. In Header upload your logo and pick the main menu. Over the hero photo the logo shows in white (Show logo in white over images); turn it off if your logo has several colors. The menu link named in Shop panel → Menu link that opens the panel (default Shop) opens a panel with your products.
  3. Set colors, font and favicon in Theme settings.
  4. Fill the homepage sections with your images and text. Every section can be hidden, reordered or removed.
  5. Create the About and Contact pages and assign the page.about and page.contact templates.

Theme settings

GroupWhat it controls
TypographyOne font for the whole store, picked from the Shopify font library. Bold and italic styles load automatically.
FaviconSquare image shown in browser tabs (at least 32 × 32 px).
LayoutMaximum page width and the minimum side margin.
ColorsBackground and text; button background and text; card background and text (photo tiles, cards, form fields); image shade and text on images (the homepage photo and anything over a photo); input corner radius.
AnimationsReveal sections on scroll: headings, cards and tiles fade up gently once. Nothing on the first screen animates, and nothing moves for visitors who turn on reduced motion.

Homepage sections

Hero

Full-screen photo with a short tagline, a line of text and a large wordmark. Optional button.

Product grid

Pick a collection or products. Color swatches switch the photo; the round button adds to the cart without leaving the page.

Details

A list of product parts (blocks). Each part has its own photo, label and text; the photo cross-fades when a part is picked.

Compare

One row per product with columns you define (blocks): typed values, metafields, product type or vendor. On phones every product becomes a card.

Lifestyle strip

A running strip of photos across a background photo or a tilted product cut-out.

Reviews mosaic

Review blocks in three columns with an average-rating card. Use real reviews only, or connect a reviews app.

FAQ

Questions as blocks. On desktop the answer shows in a panel beside the list; on phones the list opens like an accordion. Includes a contact button.

Featured product

One product with its gallery and the same blocks as the product page (title, price, variant picker, buy buttons, description, apps). Add it to any page and pick the product in the section settings.

Final call

A large heading, short text and a button, with optional product cut-outs peeking from the sides.

Pages

About

Sections: Our story (edge-to-edge photo, heading and two paragraphs), Photo list (steps or people: rows whose photo appears beside the cursor), Numbers (up to four figures that count up), Principles, Shop link and Let's talk (contact form).

Contact

Photo and contact form. Messages go to the store's sender email (Settings → Notifications). The store email line appears only when the store has one and the option is on.

Footer

Newsletter signup, up to four menus and social media icons. Show Follow on Shop adds the Shop app follow button; customers see it when Shop Pay is turned on in your store.

Policies

Shipping, refund, privacy and terms pages come from Settings → Policies and are linked in the footer automatically.

Products and cart

  • Color options use the swatch color set on the product option, or the color name.
  • The product page supports variant pickers, quantity, dynamic checkout buttons, pickup availability, gift card recipients and recommendations.
  • The cart supports notes, discounts, subscriptions and accelerated checkout buttons.

FAQ

Can I turn off the hover panel on Shop?

Yes: clear Header → Shop panel → Menu link that opens the panel.

How do I change the button color?

Theme settings → Colors → Button background and Button text.

The animations are distracting.

Uncheck Theme settings → Animations → Reveal sections on scroll.
